I want each article here to help someone make one useful decision or complete one practical step.

## Start with a task

A tool becomes easier to assess when the task is clear. What information goes in? What should come out? Who uses the result? What happens if it is wrong?

Those questions come before a recommendation.

## Show the basis for the advice

When an article reports an experiment, it should describe the inputs, setup, product versions and result. A single run remains a single run. An illustrative example should be clearly introduced as one.

A walkthrough should explain the contribution and credit the upstream work it builds on. A repository is a place to inspect an implementation; it does not establish a performance claim on its own.

## Make the next step useful

Some articles will compare ways to approach a task. Others will explain a project or examine a specific AI claim. The reader should leave knowing what to try, what to check or which question to ask next.

My personal writing is separate from my employer and its clients. Examples here use public project information or clearly identified fictional scenarios.
